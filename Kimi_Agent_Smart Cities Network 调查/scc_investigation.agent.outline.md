# Investigative Report: Smart Cities Council (SCC) and Corey Gray

## Executive Summary (~800 words)
### Key Findings
#### SCC is a for-profit private corporation, not a nonprofit or intergovernmental body as implied by its branding
#### Corey Gray's central claim of "taking LVX Global public in 2020" is demonstrably false — the company never listed and collapsed into administration in 2024
#### SCC has delivered zero independently verified physical smart city infrastructure projects
#### Governance structure is essentially nonexistent — one person controls the organization with no board oversight
#### Pattern of systematic credential inflation across multiple claims
#### Africa engagement is event-based; the "authoritarian dictator deals" allegation is unsubstantiated, but due diligence gaps are real

## 1. What SCC Actually Is — Legal Reality vs. Marketing Claims (~2500 words, 2 tables)
### 1.1 The "Social Impact Organisation" Label
#### 1.1.1 SCC is registered as a Virginia for-profit corporation (Smart Cities Council, Inc.), NOT a nonprofit — EIN 38-3981098
#### 1.1.2 Australian entity (ACN 619 159 625) is explicitly "Not entitled to receive tax deductible gifts" — also for-profit
#### 1.1.3 IRS Form 990 data shows SCC as a grant recipient with BLANK tax-exempt status — the only recipient out of 90+ with no designation
#### 1.1.4 The term "Social Impact Organisation" is not a recognized legal category in any jurisdiction — it is pure marketing

#### 1.3.2 Acquired by Design Milk Co (ASX:DMC) in May 2021 as part of a combined package with LVX Global

## 7. Conclusions and Assessment (~1500 words)
### 7.1 Is SCC a "Real" Organization?
#### 7.1.1 Yes, SCC is a legally registered, real organization — but it is not what it claims to be
#### 7.1.2 It is a membership-based events and publishing business, not an intergovernmental smart city authority
### 7.2 Is It a Money Laundering Operation?
#### 7.2.1 No evidence supports money laundering allegations — no criminal proceedings, no regulatory actions, no suspicious transaction patterns
#### 7.2.2 The more accurate characterization: a for-profit membership organization with inflated marketing claims and minimal deliverables
### 7.3 The Core Problem
#### 7.3.1 SCC exploits an accountability vacuum — not regulated as nonprofit, not publicly traded, not a government body
#### 7.3.2 The organization thrives on perception management rather than demonstrated impact
#### 7.3.3 Legitimate partners (DTC, NCS, SA Government) provide credibility by association that SCC has not independently earned
