# Investigation Plan: Smart Cities Council (SCC) & Corey Grey

## Objective
Conduct independent, evidence-based research into Smart Cities Council (SCC) and its founder/leader Corey Grey. Focus on actual activities, financial patterns, project deliveries, legal status, and credible allegations — not marketing claims.

## Stage 1 — Deep Research (Load: deep-research-swarm)
Multi-agent parallel investigation across multiple dimensions:

### Track 1: Organization Legitimacy & Legal Structure
- Company registration records, legal entity status, jurisdiction
- Board members, governance structure, accountability
- Financial records, revenue sources, funding transparency
- Tax filings and nonprofit/commercial status

### Track 2: Project Track Record & Delivery
- Claimed projects vs. independently verified completed projects
- Partners and clients — who actually worked with them
- Project outcomes, testimonials from verifiable third parties
- Geographic focus (especially Africa deals as alleged)

### Track 3: Reputation, Controversies & Red Flags
- News articles, investigative journalism, whistleblower accounts
- Legal proceedings, lawsuits, regulatory actions
- Industry forum discussions, complaints from partners or attendees
- Social media and professional network analysis

### Track 4: Corey Grey Background
- Professional history, previous ventures, track record
- Academic/professional credentials verification
- Network analysis — who he associates with
- Any past controversies or legal issues

### Track 5: Africa-Specific Investigations
- Deals with African governments or officials
- Partnerships with African entities
- Any corruption or procurement fraud allegations
- Pattern of engagement with authoritarian regimes

## Stage 2 — Synthesis & Report Writing (Load: report-writing)
Compile all findings into an investigative report with evidence grading, source attribution, and clear separation of facts vs. allegations.

## Stage 3 — Deliverable
Final report in .docx format with all evidence cited and conclusions clearly drawn from verified facts.
