# Release readiness, 25 October 2026

The task is to let a reader inspect the public ASCN record, choose the next
useful view and compare evidence without the layout manufacturing scroll or
hiding content. Preserve the existing house palette, faces, maps, charts, videos
and source material. Carry the previous communication round through a reviewed
release, and protect the same behavior when data grows.

## Found and repaired

larger examples of every primary view against the seven anti-slop rules.

The previous round's onward bands, route line, capped prose and stretched-frame
repairs are included in this release. Its separate findings remain documented in
AUDIT-2026-10-24.md.

The reviewer also found an agency-authorship claim and a promise of complete
translation in the global footer. Both contradicted the site's actual status.
The footer now states independent authorship and incomplete Thai/Chinese
translation. The library describes public downloads without claiming private
working material is open. One Chinese eight-in-ten comparison was corrected.

The expanded subview sweep also found oversized News disclosure frames,
a Regional News topic rule twice the width of its labels, and a full-width
meeting notice with only 56% content reach. Disclosure and topic bands now
fit their content. The meeting notice uses a reading-width section, factual
heading and adjacent actions where they fit. A later footer rule had placed
both disclosures in the same grid row; each now owns a responsive cell.
A three-width, three-language test checks footer intersections and bounds.

## Data growth and interaction

The table silently stopped at 200 matches while its count and CSV included all
matches. It now renders every match. A test-only dataset adds 220 rows, long prose
and a 120-character unbroken token. It checks all rows, filtered export, four
widths and cell/page containment. No fixture is published.

Language rebuilds appended duplicate project and city filter options and event
handlers. They now replace options, retain selections and replace persistent
handlers. Library search and type filters use the same idempotent approach.
Library download descriptions derive row, cycle and city counts from data.

Delayed loading could close a news record and drop keyboard focus. Evidence
renders retain open records and restore the focused control. Finishing the main
data load no longer steals phone focus or resets the reading pane. Explicit tab
navigation now adds a history entry, so Back restores the prior view. The Innovation
hero also pauses on the custom navigation event, including manually started
playback under reduced-motion preferences.

Rendered public headline and cycle-row assertions bind editorial counts to the
committed public datasets. Validation also rejects a knowledge summary that
disagrees with the roster or latest official report. A new cycle requires its dated copy and translations
to be reviewed together. No private ledger is needed by these checks.

The final deployment attempt also exposed a startup race that detached the
onward band while data finished loading. Existing bands now update in place.
A delayed-load test confirms their focused links remain usable; all four
reading-path tests pass. The failed gate stopped before upload.

## Release protection

`npm run deploy` and CI now use `npm run check:release`. The gate validates and
builds, creates a fresh Pages preview, runs browser flows and growth regressions,
runs the spatial audit in strict mode, then captures the interface. A failure
stops before upload. It retains the finished-artifact and exact-live-byte privacy
gates. The build and deployment allowlists were not broadened.

The spatial audit and captures cover all 19 routes: fifteen primary views, the
annual-meeting sheet and the three additional News panels. They wait for actual
visible map tiles. Markers on a blank map no longer pass as verified geography.
Geometry captures use the saved news fallback; live-refresh and outage behaviors
have separate browser tests. Screenshots stay local.

## Toolchain repair

The full dependency audit found three advisories through Miniflare's
`undici` 7.29.1, including a high-severity response-decoding denial of service.
The scoped Miniflare override and lockfile now use 7.30.1, the patched 7.x
release identified by the [upstream advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-xjj6-f33q-929c).
Wrangler remains 4.147.0. Only one installed package changed. The full
`npm audit --audit-level=high` reports zero vulnerabilities, and a fresh Pages
preview passes the focused zoom, showcase and navigation-playback tests.

A busy local simulator exceeded the showcase's default five-second initial-data
assertion; its trace showed successful public dataset responses. That initial
wait is now 15 seconds. The multi-view zoom and video-navigation tests have
90-second test budgets. Their content, geometry and playback assertions remain
unchanged; none are skipped or retried into a pass.

## Limits

Thai and Chinese authored prose and SVG labels remain incomplete. No native
language review is claimed. Source-issued names retain their published wording.
Source extraction artifacts require source review; they are not guessed away.
The broader graphics audit reports editorial translation debt separately from
the enforced release geometry and public-data tests. Maps depend on external
OpenStreetMap availability; tile-readiness failure blocks the release audit.

The standard deployment refresh grew the public discovery snapshot from 135
to 140 unreviewed index leads. Reviewed evidence, official portfolio data, the
public-record fingerprint, asset manifest and private material are unchanged. Historical Git and immutable snapshots are
outside this release; no history rewrite is authorised or performed.
