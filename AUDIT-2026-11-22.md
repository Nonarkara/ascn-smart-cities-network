# Composition sweep — 22 November 2026

The owner sent a screenshot of the Overview's perspective block and wrote:
*"Problems with composition here. Check everything to make sure that we don't have anything this badly composed in our system no more."*

The screenshot shows a large empty band down the **left** of the content area: the
perspective's essay column starts 184px in from the content edge, the photographs sit
beside it, and the 2px ink rule above the block still spans the whole column. The
composition promises a full-width band and then stops halfway across the screen.

This record covers the instrument that found it, every repair, every finding that was
answered with a reason instead of a repair, and the gate that keeps it from coming back.

## The instrument

`scripts/audit-composition.mjs` measures, for every routable view at 390, 1024, 1440,
1920 and 2103 px:

1. The content area is the view's own box.
2. What the reader can actually see is rasterised into a grid of 4px columns by 8px
   rows: every run of text, every graphic, every painted background, and every border
   edge as the 2px strip it actually is.
3. For each row, the stretches of columns nothing reaches.
4. A **band** is a stretch of columns that is empty in *every* row of a run of
   consecutive rows, in rows that hold content somewhere.

A margin is empty on both sides of the page; a hole is empty on one side and full on
the other. That distinction is the whole measure, and the earlier one could not make
it: it took the union of every column any leaf ever touched, so a band beside content
in one section was invisible if some other section reached across it. It found the
empty right edge of a page. It could not find the perspective's dead left band.

**Two bugs in the instrument itself, found and fixed before its first reading was
trusted:**

- *Text nodes were invisible.* The raster walked leaves only, so
  `<p><b>the claim</b> and its explanation</p>` reported the plain half as empty and
  invented a hole where the sentence was. It now walks every text node.
- *A border painted the whole box.* The edge painter filled the element's box for its
  top and left borders instead of the 2px strip, so any bordered box looked fully
  painted — and the perspective's band, which the rule above it crosses, disappeared.
  The band was "invisible" for exactly the reason it was a defect.

**What fails, and what does not.** A band fails when it is at least 12% of the content
width, at least 250px tall, and either

- it is **interior** — content on both sides of it in the same rows, so the page stops
  halfway across and then resumes; or
- a **heavy rule** (2px or more) crosses it. A rule is a claim about the width of the
  block beneath it; a 2px ink rule that spans the content area and then leaves a
  quarter of the page empty beside it is a broken promise. A hairline divider is not
  a claim and is not judged.

A band that fails neither test is a **margin**: the view is a centred band and the
space beside it is the page's own margin. Every margin above the size threshold is
listed in the report with its size, so none is silently ignored — each one is
answered below.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **The perspective — the owner's screenshot.** The pair was `46ch + 440px`, centred
   inside a 1211px column: 184px of dead page down the left of the essay and the same
   again on the right, for the 900px the block is tall, with the 2px rule above it
   spanning the whole column. It is now three compartments — the stance, the argument,
   the photographs — that take the full width. The two text columns are the measure
   itself (46ch), the photographs take what is left as a pair side by side, and the
   pull quote moved into the stance column so the three columns end within 100px of
   each other. The byline and the role moved with it, because a byline belongs with
   the person it names and the columns had to balance.
   *Measured:* 184×904 → 0. Columns at 1440: 359 / 359 / 436; at 1920: 359 / 359 / 799.

2. **Six view headers stranded their right half.** `.section-head` was capped at 52rem,
   which left 380–720px of the content area empty beside it on the History, Research,
   Framework, Partners, Insights, Data and Library views — the same stranded right
   edge the twins header had already been repaired for. The header is now two
   compartments, the title block left and the lede right, exactly as `.twins-header`
   does it. *Measured:* History 576×320 → 0; Research 592×344 → 0.

3. **The Overview's intro block.** A heading, a 359px paragraph and three buttons in a
   1574px area — more than half the row empty beside the statement. Now the statement
   left, the lede right. *Measured:* 1076×184 → 0.

4. **The Record's urban block.** A 544px table and a 359px paragraph stacked in a
   1211px column, with 764px empty beside them for the 424px the block is tall. Now
   the data (bars and table) on the left, the reading on the right, the lede across
   the top. *Measured:* 764×424 → 0.

5. **The Record's stacked chart.** A 672px chart in a 1574px column, leaving 1000px
   empty for its height. The chart and the reading it supports now sit side by side.

6. **The Record's and Innovation's row headers.** `.iaas-row-head` had the same shape
   as `.section-head` and the same stranded right half. Same repair: the statement
   left, the lede right.

7. **The Research view.** At full width each paper card held 359px of text inside a
   769px box, and the two columns of the grid floated 430px apart with a gulf between
   them; the geography grid had 230px of empty column beside every paragraph. The
   sections are now a 1020px centred band — the essay view's measure.

8. **The Essay's diagrams.** A diagram was left-aligned in a 1020px section while the
   prose sat centred in the middle 359px of it, so the chart hung off the left edge
   and the right half of the section stood empty for the height of the chart. The
   diagram now shares the prose's centre line.

9. **The Record's bar rows on a phone.** The name column reserved 128px for names that
   are 78px long at their widest, and the 44px left over ran down the column beside
   every bar. *Measured:* 44×280 → 0.

10. **The Record's urban table footnote.** Stretching the table to fill its column
    turned its last row — one line of text under the ledger — into a filled strip
    1151px wide. The spatial audit's frame check caught it at 1920 and above (3.29x
    its ink, against a 3.27x ceiling). The row is a footnote, not a slab: the wash
    is gone and the hairline above it still separates it from the data. *Measured:*
    2 spatial findings → 0.

## Findings answered with a reason, not repaired

Each is listed by the gate with its size, so none is hidden.

1. **The Essay's centred measure** (46 bands, the largest 732px × 464px at 1920). The
   essay is a reading page: one column of prose at the house's 46ch measure, centred,
   with its figures at their own width. The dividers between sections are 1px
   hairlines, not the 2px ink frame the perspective carried, so they make no claim
   about the width. A measure column with margins either side is the house's own
   long-form pattern, and the law that governs it — "wide screens get columns or
   margins, never full-width paragraphs" — asks for exactly this.
2. **The Research view's outer band** (276px × 2664px at 1920). The consequence of
   finding 7: the view is a centred 1020px band. The alternative was measured, not
   guessed: at full width the paper cards hold 359px of text in a 769px box and the
   grid's two columns float 430px apart.
3. **The News archive's records** (1008px × 280px at 1920). Each record is a headline
   and a summary in a left column, with the record's own hairline divider beneath it.
   A headline is a display element and is not measured by text area; the summary keeps
   the house measure.
4. **The Innovation view's closing note** (660px × 336px at 1920) and **the Insights
   view's chart note** (288px × 328px at 1440). A note under the thing it annotates is
   a caption, not a hole.

## Guard

- `npm run audit:composition` — the instrument and the gate. It writes
  `artifacts/composition/composition.json` with every failure and every margin.
- `tests/composition.spec.mjs` — the falsifier. A gate that cannot fail on the defect
  it was written for is not evidence, so the test plants the owner's defect (the
  perspective as a narrow centred pair again) and a second one (a gulf inside a
  full-width row), and asserts the instrument reports both. It also asserts the
  shipped Overview is clean.
- Both are wired into `npm run check:release`, which `npm run deploy` runs before it
  uploads anything.

## Evidence that the gate can fail

A gate that cannot fail on the defect it was written for is not evidence. The
falsifier plants the owner's defect — the perspective as a narrow centred pair, with
the 2px rule above it still spanning the column — and a second defect, a gulf
inside a full-width row. The instrument reports both, and reports the shipped
Overview as clean. The gate's thresholds were not widened at any point to make a
release pass.

## Limits

- The screenshots behind this pass were reviewed by an independent reviewer and by
  the author of the changes; the numbers are the instrument's, not a reader's.
- The 2103px viewport was added because the owner's own screen is that wide and the
  original instrument stopped at 1440px, where the perspective's band was narrower.
- A margin is judged by rule and reason, not by eye. The reasoning is written down
  above so it can be disagreed with.
- The Research view's band is a trade-off, and it is the one judgement in this pass
  that a reasonable owner could reverse: a centred 1020px band against full width
  with hollow paper cards. The alternative was measured, not guessed — at full width
  each card holds 359px of text in a 769px box and the grid's two columns float
  430px apart.
