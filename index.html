About

Incentives over instructions. Data with empathy.

Established 28 April 2018 at the 32nd ASEAN Summit. Anchored by the Smart Cities Framework adopted 13 November 2018. From 26 pilot cities to 38 by January 2026.

City-to-city learning, bankable project pipelines, better service delivery. A regional operating platform and shared urban learning environment.

Timeline

  1. 2018
    Network launched at 32nd ASEAN Summit. 26 pilot cities. Framework adopted at 33rd Summit.
  2. 2019
    Thailand chair year. Implementation-focused coordination.
  3. 2022
    Planning Guidebook. Clearer process, priority areas, enabling conditions.
  4. 2025
    Malaysia hosts ASCN 8. Action Plan 2026-2035 begins.
  5. 2026
    Philippines chairship. Network reaches 38 cities with Dili.
