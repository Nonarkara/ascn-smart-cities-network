# ASCN News Evidence Review, 8 October 2026

Question: what does publicly accessible coverage establish about ASCN's cooperation, bankable-project and external-funding objectives, and where does it leave delivery or citizen impact unverified?

The structured record is `data/ascn-news-archive.json`; search discoveries are `data/ascn-news-discovery.json`. The latter are not performance evidence. Searches used the full network name by year (2018–2026), annual meetings, funding, implementation, outcomes, member-city names and Indonesian/Thai name variants. Later passes also read Malay, Japanese and Vietnamese reporting, consolidated action plans, and ADB, World Bank, DFAT, US-ASEAN and ASEAN-Korea project pages. The source hierarchy includes original ASEAN and national-government statements, donor/implementing-partner records, news reporting, and attributed research/commentary. Source type does not guarantee independence: ANTARA, AKP, RTM, VietnamPlus, BERNAMA and the Global New Light of Myanmar are state-linked publishers; implementers report their own work.

Source links, access method, language, publication date or unknown-date marker, context year, event key, reviewed claim, interpretation, evidence needed, city/project links and metrics are kept with each record. The public ledger links back to the publisher. No article bodies or publisher photographs are copied.

## What Changed the Review

- Australia's 2018 regional investment announcement is a commitment, not a disbursement to each member city.
- Siem Reap's eight pilots and six being implemented/expanded are reported outputs. AKP and JICA describe the same programme; these are two records, one topic/event, and not two independent impact evaluations.
- Makassar's patient reach and Jakarta's registrations show use of services. They do not measure health improvement, business survival or ASCN's causal contribution.
- The Indonesian December 2025 presentation includes candidate cities. The official roster, not attendance or a headline, determines which cities this system recognises as members.
- A member roster is an institutional output. Mentioning Dili in it cannot count as local project delivery. City delivery coverage requires a linked city project.
- The 2025 M&E totals remain an official, separately sourced baseline. News classifications never rewrite status. Missing extracted appendix rows are not zero projects.
- An October 2026 pass added sources that were fetched and read: the 2018 consolidated action plans, the 2022 AASCTF mid-term review, ADB project records for Luang Prabang and Vientiane, the World Bank's Da Nang completion report, the December 2020 Smart JAMP white-paper column, a U.S.-ASEAN partnership overview, ASEAN-Korea training notes, and city reporting from VietnamPlus, Utusan, The Star/Bernama, BERNAMA, Build4People, the Global New Light of Myanmar, Ho Chi Minh City's digital centre and UN-Habitat. Discovery-index leads were not copied in again.
- None of those additions is an archive outcome. Partial disbursement, construction progress, a pre-launch, a data-centre launch, enrolment and a Bank project rating stay in their own stages. The Da Nang rating is the Bank's results-framework judgement of a project approved in 2013; it is not treated as an ASCN citizen-outcome comparison.
- Where a source names a programme that does not exactly match a 2025 register title, the city can still be cited and the project row is left unlinked. Luang Prabang's ADB loan is the clearest case.

## Limits and Revision Rule

This is a growing public-evidence archive, not every article ever published. Search engines limit and reorder matches; paywalls, deleted links, local languages and reporting bias affect coverage. Two ASEAN releases were available through indexed text when direct access failed and are labelled accordingly. Missing news is not missing activity. Hosting a regional meeting does not establish host-city performance.

This selection still contains no event with an explicit reviewed citizen-outcome comparison. That is a statement about this selection, not a conclusion that ASCN delivered no benefits. Official releases, donor promotion and English-accessible sources remain overrepresented, including after Japanese, Vietnamese and Malay additions. More resident evidence, critical reporting and comparable before-and-after measures are needed before any network-wide verdict.

A stronger finding requires dated, linked evidence: independently checked operating assets; finance actually disbursed or financial close; comparable service measures before and after delivery; inclusion and distributional measures; and a credible basis for attributing change. Such evidence can change the review. Critiques are attributed questions, not accusations. Added outcome records must identify their comparison basis and reported metrics.

The index endpoint refreshes fixed name/variant searches on request with edge caching. `npm run refresh:ascn-news` appends discoveries to the committed fallback without discarding history. An editor must inspect the original source before adding a reviewed claim. Review classifications, source dates and uncertainties travel with CSV exports and the archive JSON.
